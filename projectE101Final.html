Overview

This project was the final for my very first engineering class, which I was later a TA for. The aim of this peoject was to teach AI literacy to middle school aged children, and was requested from real Indiana teachers.

My Role

This project was intended to be done in groups of three, but I was a part of the only group of two. My partner and I split portions of the work, and I was mainly responsible for the coding and technical aspects of the project.

Technical Details

  • Tools: Raspberry Pi, Python
  • Concepts: Machine Learning, Python Programming, Means Trees
  • Impact: My partner and I were two of the three teaching assistants for the next semester. Our work was also presented to middle schoolers and showcased at a local education conference.
