Overview

I am currently working on a PFC inverter project through my research position at the University of Minnesota. The goal of this project is to design a high-efficiency PFC converter that can be used in a variety of applications, including electric vehicles and renewable energy systems. The project involves simulation, control loop design, and hardware implementation.

My Role

I joined this project back in October 2025, and I have been working on it since it was just a concept. I began working in this project by creating documentation for OpenDSS, a simulation software for power electronics. The goal was to model simulations from Arizona State University and and compare them to simulation results. This all pivoted around November, when we reached out to the Microgrid Lab at the Universit of St. Thomas, and now we have access to the world's largest microgrid to do our testing. Since this shift, I have been modeling simulations in Matlab and Simulink, and I have been working on the control loop design for the PFC converter. I have also made a PCB design for the High Voltage sensing board on our PFC converter. We are currently in the process of adding all boards together, and will begin manufacturing in the upcoming semester.

Technical Details

  • Tools: Altium Designer, MATLAB, Simulink
  • Concepts: Power Electronics, Control Systems, Microgrids
  • Impact: Making a tabletop sized PFC inverter.
